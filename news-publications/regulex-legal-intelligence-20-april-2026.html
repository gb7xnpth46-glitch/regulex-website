Regulex Legal Intelligence - 20 April 2026

April 2026 By Oleg Pikalov, Managing Partner

Corporate & Regulatory Updates

New Tax Penalties Hit April 14

What happened: New UAE tax penalty rules take effect on April 14, 2026, replacing the previous penalty tables from 2017. The changes include a new voluntary disclosure penalty of 1% per month on the tax difference, while penalties for failing to update taxpayer information dropped to AED 1,000 for first violations and AED 5,000 for repeat violations.

Why it matters: Higher penalties if you're late on voluntary disclosures but lower fines for admin mistakes.

What to do: Review your tax controls now and ensure VAT filings are always on time.

Case of the Week

DIFC Court Rejects $31 Million CEO Employment Claim

What happened: Ex-ArcelorMittal CEO Paramjit Kahlon sued Liberty Steel Group for $31 million in bonuses and incentives, claiming he was fraudulently deceived about his employment terms. DIFC Court dismissed all claims on February 26, 2026.

Why it matters: Court confirmed that employment contracts don't need every detail finalized to be binding - but you still have to meet the actual performance targets to get paid.

What to do: Include express governing law clauses in DIFC contracts - otherwise DIFC law applies automatically, which may not be what you expected.

International Developments

FATF Grey List Adds Kuwait and Papua New Guinea

What happened: FATF added Kuwait and Papua New Guinea to its grey list in February 2026. Kuwait made a high-level political commitment to strengthen its AML/CFT regime after failures to demonstrate effective implementation despite technical progress.

Why it matters: Enhanced due diligence now required for transactions with these countries. UAE businesses may face additional compliance checks when dealing with Kuwaiti entities or financial institutions.

What to do: Update your sanctions and compliance screening systems immediately. Review any business relationships with these jurisdictions.

Iran-US Sanctions Relief Talks Stall

What happened: President Trump announced a two-week ceasefire with Iran on April 7, 2026, but weekend negotiations between Vice President JD Vance and Iran failed to reach agreement.

Why it matters: Trump announced a "complete blockade" on the Strait of Hormuz after talks ended. This affects oil prices and supply chains for UAE businesses importing goods through this critical route.

What to do: Review your supply chain exposure to Hormuz transit. Consider alternative shipping routes and monitor energy cost impacts on operations.

Fun Legal Fact

In 1998, a British man legally changed his name to "They" to avoid paying council tax, arguing the bills were addressed to someone who didn't exist. The council wasn't amused and continued pursuing payment. After years of legal wrangling, They (the person) eventually lost the case and had to pay up. Turns out you can't outsmart bureaucracy with grammar - who knew?
