Short answer

Offshore company formation in the UAE means registering a company with RAK International Corporate Centre (RAK ICC) in Ras Al Khaimah or the Jebel Ali Free Zone Authority (JAFZA) in Dubai under that registry's regulations; Ajman Free Zone also lists an offshore licence. At RAK ICC and JAFZA the company is normally formed through a registered agent approved by the registry. It may hold shares, bank accounts and, in designated areas, real estate, but registration is not a trade licence. It is incorporated under UAE legislation: corporate tax under Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022, the beneficial owner register under Cabinet Decision No. 109 of 2023 and anti-money-laundering rules apply.

What is an offshore company in the UAE?

JAFZA's regulations call the entity an Offshore Company; RAK ICC calls it an international business company (IBC).

Regulation 1(3) of the RAK ICC Business Companies Regulations 2018 excludes the federal Commercial Companies Law; regulation 4.3 of the Jebel Ali Free Zone Offshore Companies Regulations 2023 leaves it only for matters they do not cover.

An offshore company is still incorporated under UAE legislation, so the federal rules below apply. JAFZA's guide lists its typical uses: international trade, holding companies, owning real estate, registering copyrights and patents, and international consulting.

Of these, offshore company formation in Dubai is a matter for JAFZA and a RAK offshore company for RAK ICC. In Abu Dhabi, ADGM, a financial free zone, registers special purpose vehicles, which its site describes as passive holding companies that cannot conduct operational business or hire staff.

What can and can't an offshore company do?

What JAFZA regulation 14.2 expressly allows (RAK ICC regulation 40(6) has a similar list for its own zone):

  • hold shares in other companies, including a company that operates in the UAE;
  • hold a bank account in the UAE;
  • own property in designated freehold areas (JAFZA). For RAK ICC companies owned by natural persons, the Dubai Land Department agreed in 2019 to register freehold property against a no-objection letter.

What the regulations do not allow:

  • Trading without a licence. A JAFZA Offshore Company needs licences or permits from the competent authorities for business activities in the zone or elsewhere (regulation 14.3). A RAK ICC company may not do business with persons in its zone unless RAK ICC authorises it (regulation 40(5)) and needs the competent authorities' licences for activities elsewhere in the UAE (regulation 40(8)).
  • Financial services. A RAK ICC company may not provide banking, insurance, investment or similar financial services by way of business (regulation 40(5) and (7)).
  • Bearer shares. Prohibited by JAFZA regulation 22, RAK ICC regulation 50 and Article 11 of Cabinet Decision No. 109 of 2023.
  • An office of its choice. A RAK ICC company's registered office is normally its agent's office in the UAE (regulation 91); a JAFZA company's may be in the zone, at a freehold property it owns or at its agent's office in Dubai (regulation 31.1).
  • Visas as a matter of course. The RAK ICC regulations do not mention visas. A JAFZA Offshore Company that owns property in a designated freehold area may apply for residency visas for its members, subject to the Authority's eligibility rules (regulation 31.2).

Which UAE rules apply to an offshore company anyway?

Corporate tax. A juridical person incorporated under UAE legislation is a Resident Person and so a Taxable Person, taxed on income from the UAE and from abroad (Articles 11 and 12 of Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022). A Taxable Person must register (Article 51); for companies incorporated on or after 1 March 2024 the deadline is three months from incorporation (FTA Decision No. 3 of 2024). A tax return is due within nine months of the end of each tax period (Article 53). The standard rates are 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000 and 9% above it (u.ae); Articles 22 and 23 exempt some categories of income. The 0% free-zone rate is reserved for a Qualifying Free Zone Person, which must, among other conditions, maintain adequate substance (Article 18): core activities, assets and full-time employees in a Free Zone (FTA guide). How this applies to a particular offshore company is a question for a tax adviser.

Beneficial owner register. Cabinet Decision No. 109 of 2023 (listed by the Ministry of Economy and Tourism) applies to legal persons registered in the UAE, including in commercial free zones; financial free zones are exempt (Article 3). A beneficial owner is the natural person who ultimately holds 25% or more of the capital or votes, or controls the company by other means (Article 5). The company keeps registers of beneficial owners and of shareholders, files them with the registrar within 60 days of registration and records changes within 15 days (Articles 8, 10 and 11). Cabinet Resolution No. 134 of 2025, the executive regulations of the anti-money-laundering law below, gives companies 15 working days to update beneficial owner information (Article 38). The two periods differ; the registrar can say which one it applies.

Anti-money-laundering rules. Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025 repealed the 2018 law (Article 41). Banks and designated non-financial businesses must run customer due diligence (Article 19); false information about a beneficial owner is a criminal offence (Article 35). RAK ICC will not register a company where due diligence cannot be completed.

Economic substance. Cabinet Decision No. 98 of 2024 cancelled notifications and reports for financial years ending after 31 December 2022. Obligations for earlier years remain.

How offshore company formation in the UAE works: the steps

  1. Check that the planned purpose needs no licence.
  2. Choose the registry and a registered agent it has approved.
  3. Pass the agent's due diligence on identity, beneficial owners and source of funds.
  4. The application is filed with the memorandum and articles; at RAK ICC only the agent may file it (regulation 6).
  5. The registry issues the certificate of incorporation.
  6. File the beneficial owner data, register for corporate tax and apply for a bank account.

Checklist before you register an offshore company

  • The planned activity needs no trade licence, office or visas.
  • The registry's rules on property and shareholdings fit the assets.
  • Beneficial owners are identified down to natural persons.
  • Source-of-funds documents are ready for the agent and the bank.
  • The shareholders' agreement matches the articles.
  • A tax adviser has reviewed the structure.
  • The corporate tax registration deadline is in the calendar.
  • Annual duties are budgeted: renewal, accounts, tax return and, for JAFZA, an audit (regulation 6.2).

FAQ

Can an offshore company trade in the UAE?

Not on its registration alone. An offshore company in Dubai registered with JAFZA needs licences or permits for business activities (regulation 14.3). It may own a stake in a UAE operating company (regulation 14.2).

Can an offshore company open a bank account in the UAE?

JAFZA regulation 14.2 allows an account in a UAE bank, and RAK ICC regulation 40(6) permits one in its zone. An offshore company with a bank account in Dubai or another emirate is not a given: the bank decides after due diligence under Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025.

How much does a RAK offshore company cost?

The RAK ICC fee schedule effective 1 January 2026 sets the basic registry fee, subject to change without notice, at AED 3,250 for a one-year incorporation and AED 3,950 for a one-year renewal. Agent, accounting and tax costs are extra. For the cost of an Ajman offshore company, ask Ajman Free Zone; its site publishes no offshore fees.

Does an offshore company pay corporate tax in the UAE?

As a company incorporated under UAE legislation it is a Taxable Person, with duties to register and file returns (Articles 11, 51 and 53 of Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022). Whether any tax is payable depends on its income and the exemptions it meets: a question for a tax adviser.
