Why real estate companies in the UAE need ongoing legal support

The rules sit in layers. UAE real estate law combines federal law, emirate legislation and separate regimes in the DIFC and ADGM. Dubai and Abu Dhabi each have their own real estate regulator.

The rules moved in 2025–2026. A new federal Civil Transactions Law replaced the 1985 law on 1 June 2026 (promulgating decree, Arabic text). It requires good faith in pre-contract negotiations (Article 121) and disclosure of information decisive to the other party's consent (Article 122). A new anti-money laundering law took effect on 14 October 2025. Dubai's new real estate laws of 2026 include Law No. (3) of 2026 on the quality and safety of buildings, and Law No. (4) of 2026 on shared housing (Arabic text; English press summary).

Registration matters. In Dubai, Article 3 of Law No. (13) of 2008 provides that an off-plan sale not entered in the Interim Property Register is void. Bylaw No. (85) of 2006 provides for a broker's licence and entry in the Brokers Register (Article 3).

The legal work that comes up every month

  • Brokers. Brokerage agreements, which Bylaw 85 requires to be in writing (Article 26); commission-sharing terms; developer marketing agreements, which Law 13 of 2008 requires the developer to register with DLD (Article 9).
  • Developers. Off-plan sale agreements, escrow agreements and management agreements for common areas.
  • Property managers. Management mandates, facilities contracts and tenancy contracts. The Dubai tenancy law as amended provides for 90 days' notice before expiry to change lease terms unless agreed otherwise (Article 14) and 12 months' notice, through a notary public or registered mail, for eviction on expiry on listed grounds (Article 25).
  • Holiday-home operators. Owner–operator agreements, guest terms and platform contracts.
  • Landlords, occupiers and groups. Office and retail leases; questions such as which entity holds the asset.

Checklist before you sign a legal retainer with a real estate lawyer in Dubai

  • Check the provider's UAE licence. Onshore court representation is for lawyers on the Roll of Practising Lawyers (Article 6 of Federal Decree-Law No. (34) of 2022); legal consultants advise and draft.
  • Get the scope in writing: document types and volume.
  • Read the exclusions: onshore litigation, tax, audits.
  • Confirm the fee, VAT and what counts as extra work.
  • Ask for the response time, who reviews documents and how AI is used.
  • Ask about conflicts with your counterparties.
  • Check the term and how to cancel.

FAQ

What is a retainer fee for a lawyer?

A periodic fee agreed for a set scope of work, instead of payment per hour. Subscription legal services work the same way.

What does the RERA rent increase law in Dubai allow?

Decree No. (43) of 2013 provides for capped increases at renewal: none if rent is up to 10% below the average for similar units, then 5%, 10%, 15% and 20% bands as the gap widens. It uses the rent index approved by RERA (Article 3). DLD's Smart Rental Index (January 2025) covers residential areas.

What does Dubai property law for foreigners permit?

Article 4 of Law No. (7) of 2006 reserves ownership for UAE and GCC nationals, their wholly owned companies and public joint stock companies. In areas designated by the Ruler, it allows non-UAE nationals freehold title, or usufruct or leasehold for up to 99 years. This is the core of Dubai freehold property law; Regulation No. (3) of 2006 lists the first areas.

Which jointly owned property law applies in Dubai?

Law No. (6) of 2019. By its terms it covers free zones too and gives the Rental Disputes Center jurisdiction over its disputes (Articles 3 and 42).

How do you choose a real estate lawyer in Dubai?

Compare providers on licence, scope, response time and who does the work. This guide ranks no one as the best, and REGULEX does not describe itself that way. A retainer suits recurring contracts; a court or Rental Disputes Center claim needs court representation, which REGULEX does not provide.
