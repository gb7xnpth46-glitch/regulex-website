Why agencies in the UAE need ongoing marketing legal services

The regulator changed. Federal Decree-Law No. 11 of 2025 set up the National Media Authority (NMA) from 1 January 2026. It replaced the UAE Media Council (Article 13); earlier Council resolutions stay in force until replaced (Article 16).

The media law regulates advertising. Federal Decree-Law No. 55 of 2023 Regulating Media applies to persons, establishments and media free zones (Article 2) and requires advertising to be clearly identified as such (Article 20). It provides for administrative fines from AED 1,000 to AED 1,000,000, doubled on repetition up to AED 2,000,000 (Article 23).

Influencers generally need a permit. Article 12 of the media law provides for a permit for individuals who publish advertising on social media, paid or unpaid. The NMA's Advertiser Permit page lists the conditions: citizens and residents also need a trade licence for electronic media; visitors register through an approved advertising or talent management agency. It exempts individuals advertising their own products, or their own company's, through personal accounts. Check the NMA's current page before relying on a condition.

Some breaches are criminal. Promoting goods or services online through a misleading advertisement carries imprisonment and/or a fine of AED 20,000 to AED 500,000 under Article 48 of Federal Decree-Law No. 34 of 2021 on Countering Rumors and Cybercrimes.

The layers differ. Federal law is the base, emirates add requirements, and DIFC and ADGM have their own data protection rules.

The legal work that comes up every month

  • Agency–client agreements. Scope, approvals, responsibility for product claims, liability and ownership. Under the copyright law, a transfer of economic rights must be in writing and state the right, purpose, duration and place (Article 9). Unless agreed otherwise in writing, copyright in a work made in favour of another person is attributed to that person (Article 28).
  • Agency–influencer agreements. Permit warranties, disclosure of the paid relationship (Article 33 of the Executive Regulation), content approval, usage rights and exclusivity.
  • Campaign review. Claims checked against false advertising laws and deceptive advertising laws: Article 17 of the Consumer Protection Law, Article 8 of its Executive Regulations and the media regulation's sector approvals (Article 33).
  • Promotions and prize draws. Article 18 of the Consumer Protection Law requires a prior licence from the competent authority for promotions and discounts. In Dubai, retailers running festival promotions, including raffles, apply for a certificate of participation; other periods and emirates are not covered here.
  • Data and suppliers. Consent wording, processor terms with ad-tech vendors, freelancer and production agreements, NDAs.

FAQ

Is network marketing legal in UAE?

This guide gives no yes-or-no answer: no official statement on it was found. On 14 November 2026 an English keyword search of the federal legislation platform for "network marketing", "multi-level marketing", "pyramid" and "direct selling" returned no results, which is not a legal position: other rules may apply, and a scheme needs legal review. Separately, Cabinet Resolution No. 56 of 2024 bars natural persons from making marketing calls from numbers registered in their own name (Article 3).

What does UAE social media influencer law require from an agency?

The NMA's Advertiser Permit page describes an individual's permit: the holder displays its number and publishes only through registered accounts. For agencies, Article 27 of the Executive Regulation lists advertising design, production and consultancy among licensed media activities, and Article 33 requires paid endorsements to be disclosed. The agency's own licensing position needs legal review.

Which email marketing laws and telemarketing laws apply?

On 14 November 2026 an English search of the federal legislation platform for "email marketing" and "spam" returned no results; TDRA's FAQs cover marketing SMS only. Other rules still apply: the personal data law requires consent unless an exception applies and lets a person stop processing for direct marketing. Calls and marketing texts fall under Cabinet Resolution No. 56 of 2024, with penalties in Resolution No. 57.

Do UAE advertising laws apply to the agency or only to the brand?

The texts are not limited to the brand. Article 17 of the Consumer Protection Law addresses the advertiser, the provider and the commercial agent; Article 33 of the Executive Regulation addresses persons, establishments and media institutions, including in media free zones. How responsibility is split in a given campaign needs legal review.

What is a retainer fee for a lawyer?

In this guide, a fixed periodic fee for an agreed scope of legal work. Legal subscription services cover part of the role of general counsel: contracts, memos and research, not court work.
