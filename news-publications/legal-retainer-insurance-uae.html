Why insurance companies in the UAE need ongoing legal support

  • The statute changed in 2025. Federal Decree-Law No. 6 of 2025 took effect on 16 September 2025 and repealed Federal Decree-Law No. 48 of 2023 and the 2018 Central Bank law (Article 185). Earlier regulations stay in force until replaced (Article 183). Businesses were given one year from that date to align, a period the Central Bank's Board may extend (Article 184; Central Bank FAQ).
  • The licence perimeter is wide. No one may carry on a licensed financial activity without a licence (Article 60). Insurance-related professions include agents, brokers, surveyors and loss adjusters, consultants, actuaries and health insurance third-party administrators (Article 1).
  • Onshore and financial free zones are separate markets. The Decree-Law does not apply in the financial free zones (Article 2). Property and liabilities in the UAE are insured with UAE-licensed insurers, and a company may not insure its UAE staff with an insurer abroad or in a financial free zone, subject to exceptions (Article 82). DIFC firms face a corresponding restriction in DFSA COB 7.2.2.
  • Disputes go to a committee first. Court claims arising from insurance contracts are not accepted unless the dispute was first presented to the dispute committees (Article 148).
  • Health cover is regulated by emirate, with separate rules for Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the other emirates.

The legal work that comes up every month

  • Brokerage agreements with insurers, client terms of business and commission arrangements.
  • Agency, third-party administrator, provider-network, reinsurance broking and distribution agreements.
  • Insurtech contracts: platform terms, aggregator, referral, white-label and API agreements, outsourcing and cloud contracts.
  • Policy wording review for corporate buyers: group medical, liability, property or cargo cover. A cargo owner looking for a marine insurance lawyer often needs one clause read before signing.
  • Written questions: does this activity need a Central Bank licence, can an onshore risk be placed through a DIFC firm, what do employer health insurance laws require in each emirate.
  • Claim files: a memo on the policy position before the company files a complaint, often the first thing needed by companies searching for an insurance claim lawyer or insurance dispute lawyer.

Insurance legal advice for a business usually means this: contracts, memos and research.

Checklist before you sign a legal retainer

  • The legal retainer agreement lists the included work, not only the fee.
  • Exclusions are written down: court litigation, tax, compliance audits.
  • You know the monthly volume the fee assumes.
  • The response time to a request is stated.
  • A lawyer reviews every document; use of AI tools is disclosed.
  • Confidentiality terms cover policyholder and claims data.
  • The adviser has no conflict with the insurers you place business with.
  • Cancellation terms are clear.

FAQ

What do insurance lawyers do for a broker or an insurtech?

For a business, an insurance lawyer mostly reviews and drafts agreements, checks whether an activity needs a licence and explains what a regulation requires. Court representation is outside the REGULEX subscription.

Which health insurance law applies to employers in Dubai and Abu Dhabi?

In Dubai, Law No. 11 of 2013, the DHA insurance law, makes employers enrol employees, bear the cost and keep cover valid during employment (Article 10). In Abu Dhabi, under Abu Dhabi Health Insurance Law No. 23 of 2005, employers cover employees and their family members (the employee's wife and three children under 18), and sponsors cover those the employer does not (Article 5).

Is there a new health insurance law in Abu Dhabi or the UAE?

In Abu Dhabi, the Department of Health applied Law No. 23 of 2005 in a circular of 16 March 2026. What people call new health insurance laws are federal measures. Since 1 January 2025 employers in the five northern emirates must buy a policy to issue or renew a private-sector employee's residency permit (MOHRE). On 19 May 2026 the state news agency WAM reported a presidential directive to adopt a national healthcare system for citizens, underpinned by a national health insurance scheme covering all emirates (WAM), our only source for it.

What do health insurance cancellation laws say when an employee leaves?

In Dubai, a policy expires when the coverage period ends or the relationship with the employer ends; after a residence permit is cancelled, cover continues for the period set by law (Law No. 11 of 2013, Article 17). Under the basic scheme, the second-year premium can be refunded if the visa is cancelled (MOHRE).

Where does an insurance dispute go before court?

The complaint goes to the insurer first. After the waiting period stated on Sanadak's eligibility page, consumers and small and medium enterprises may go to Sanadak and escalate to its Insurance Dispute Resolution Committee. Committee decisions bind the insurer in disputes up to AED 100,000; above that, either side may challenge them before the Court of Appeal within 30 days (Federal Decree-Law No. 6 of 2025, Article 148).
