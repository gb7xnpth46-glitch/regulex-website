Short answer

In the UAE private sector, a labour contract (also spelled "labor contract"; official sources also say "employment contract") is concluded for a fixed term, on a form held in the system of the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE), in Arabic plus a language the employee understands. The rules are in Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 Regarding the Regulation of Employment Relationships and Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022. Probation is capped at six months, notice runs from 30 to 90 days, a non-compete cannot last more than two years, and a clause that gives the employee less than the law is void. The registered contract can be viewed through the ministry's View Approved Contract service. The DIFC and the ADGM have their own rules.

Labour contract in the UAE: which law applies to your company

  • Federal law. Article 3 of the Decree-Law applies it to all establishments, employers and workers in the UAE private sector. Government employees, the armed forces, police and security, and domestic workers are outside it. On the Dubai mainland, a "Dubai labour contract" is the federal contract.
  • Free zones. Article 3 makes no exception for free zones (on the DIFC and ADGM, see below). The government portal adds that employees there follow their free zone authority's rules and that contract terms must accord with the federal law (u.ae, Working in free zones). For a free zone employment contract, check the authority's own procedure and forms. DMCC states that employment in its zone is governed by the federal law and provides DMCC employment contract templates (DMCC Employment FAQs, December 2022).
  • DIFC and ADGM. A DIFC employment contract falls under the DIFC Employment Law, DIFC Law No. 2 of 2019: written, in English, given within seven days of the start date (Article 14). An ADGM employment contract falls under the Employment Regulations 2024, in force since 1 April 2025 (ADGM Employment Affairs Office): written, signed by both sides, with a copy to the employee within one month (ADGM guidance). Both centres allow probation of up to six months and do not require a fixed term.

The ministry's consolidated text shows amendments by Federal Decree-Law No. 14 of 2022 (contract term) and No. 9 of 2024; the government portal also lists No. 20 of 2023. We found no later amendment to the contract rules on 21 November 2026.

What must an employment contract in the UAE contain?

Article numbers below refer to the ministry's consolidated text of the Decree-Law and of Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022 ("the Regulation").

How do the offer letter and the registered contract fit together?

The government portal says the job offer is provided in Arabic, English and a third language the worker understands, with an annex summarising the law. The signed offer is attached to the work permit application (u.ae, Job offers and the employment process).

The contract is based on that offer. The ministry says it may add benefits beyond those in the employment offer letter, and appendices that do not conflict with the law (MOHRE, Dear Worker).

Registration is the employer's task, through the ministry's Issuance/Renewal of Employment Contracts service:

  1. Log in with UAE Pass.
  2. Enter the data and attach the contract signed by both parties.
  3. Workers in skill levels 5 to 9 attend a guidance workshop.
  4. Pay the fees.
  5. The contract is approved electronically.

The deadline for submitting a new contract runs from the employee's entry or change of status; check the current figure on the service page.

A sample or template of a UAE labour contract found online does not replace the ministry's form.

What can an employer not put in the contract?

  • An open-ended term (Article 8(3)).
  • Probation or notice outside the limits in the table above. Notice must be the same for both parties unless the difference serves the employee (Articles 9 and 43).
  • Recruitment or employment costs charged to the employee, directly or indirectly (Article 6(4)).
  • A non-compete outside the limits in the table. It falls away if the employer ends the contract in breach of the law (Article 10).
  • A right to hold the employee's official documents (Article 13(2)).
  • A right to move the employee to fundamentally different work without written consent, outside the temporary exception in Article 12.
  • A waiver of statutory rights. Terms that conflict with the law are void unless they are more favourable to the employee (Article 65(3)).

Breaches with no specific penalty are punishable by a fine of AED 5,000 to AED 1,000,000 (Article 63).

How are changes, addenda and renewal handled?

An addendum to an employment contract needs the employee's agreement and the ministry's approval, and must not reduce the employee's rights, according to the government portal. The employer files the change through Modification of Work Permits / Employment Contracts. A move to another work pattern also requires payment of what is due under the original contract (Regulation, Article 10(3)).

On employment contract renewal, a renewed term counts as continuous service (Article 8). If both sides keep working after expiry without a new agreement, the contract is extended on the same terms.

Expiry of the term ends the contract unless it is extended or renewed (Article 42(2)). Articles 8 and 42 set no form for a non-renewal of contract letter. Because continued work extends the contract, an employer that will not renew has reason to say so in writing before the end date; the contract's own terms may require more.

How to check a labour contract registered with the ministry

The ministry's View Approved Contract service is listed under both labour and employer services.

  1. Open the service page and choose Start Service.
  2. On the Employment offers and contracts services page, go to "View my contract".
  3. Search by Emirates ID number or by transaction number. The transaction search also asks for passport number, nationality, date of birth and the code shown on screen.
  4. Open the contract and compare it with your signed copy.

Checklist before you issue or renew a contract

  • Confirm the layer: federal law, a free zone authority's procedure, the DIFC or the ADGM. The points below follow the federal law.
  • Match the contract to the signed offer letter; benefits may be added, not removed.
  • State a fixed term, the work pattern and every item in Article 10(1) of the Regulation.
  • Keep probation, notice and any non-compete agreement within the limits in the table.
  • Check that the Arabic and the second-language text say the same thing.
  • Register the contract, give the employee a copy and file later changes through the ministry.
  • Diarise the expiry date and decide on renewal in writing.

FAQ

Can an employment contract in the UAE be for an unlimited term?

Not under the federal law. Article 8(3) of Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 requires a specified term, and Article 68 told employers to convert older unlimited contracts (u.ae). The DIFC and the ADGM do not require a fixed term.

What limits apply to a non-compete clause?

It must be limited by time, place and type of work, and cannot exceed two years after the contract ends (Article 10 of the federal Decree-Law). Under Article 12 of the Regulation the employer carries the burden of proving the damage, and the clause does not apply if the contract ends during probation.

How do I check my labour contract in the UAE?

Use the ministry's View Approved Contract service. The search runs on an Emirates ID number, or on a transaction number with passport details.

Does a contract in the DIFC or the ADGM go to the ministry?

No. The ADGM guidance states that ADGM, as a financial free zone, is exempt from the federal labour law and from MOHRE resolutions. A DIFC contract follows the DIFC Employment Law, which sets its own contract requirements (Article 14).
