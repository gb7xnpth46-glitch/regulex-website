Why construction companies in the UAE need ongoing legal support

UAE construction law has no single code. It combines federal law, emirate licensing rules and the contract.

The federal rules changed on 1 June 2026. The new Civil Transactions Law repeals the 1985 law (Article 2 of the promulgating decree, Arabic text) and regulates the construction contract (muqawala) in Articles 812–839. Where an engineer designs a building and supervises the contractor, both are, as a rule, jointly liable for ten years from handover for collapse and for defects that threaten structural safety (Article 821). A clause excluding or limiting that liability is void (Article 823), and a warranty claim is inadmissible three years after the collapse or discovery of the defect (Article 824). Templates that cite articles of the 1985 law need checking. The law does not apply retroactively to earlier facts and acts unless the law provides otherwise (Article 4); what that means for a contract signed before June 2026 needs advice on that contract.

Dubai now registers and classifies contractors. Law No. (7) of 2025 covers contractors across the emirate, including free zones and the DIFC, except airport-related works (Article 4). Contracting needs a trade licence and entry in Dubai Municipality's register (Article 5). The law took effect in January 2026, six months after publication (Article 29); existing contractors have one year from then to regularise, extendable by the committee (Article 26).

Cash flow depends on wording. Under a letter of guarantee, the bank may not refuse payment for reasons arising from the underlying contract, unless a court order attaches the amount (Article 417 of the Commercial Transactions Law). Under a lump-sum contract on an agreed design, the contractor cannot claim more because materials or wages rose, unless a court rebalances the contract in unforeseeable exceptional circumstances (Article 829 of the Civil Transactions Law).

The legal work that comes up every month

Articles cited are from the federal Civil Transactions Law.

  • Main contracts and subcontracts. Construction contract law starts with scope, quantity, method, time and price, which the contract must state (Article 813). A construction contract lawyer checks particular conditions against them.
  • Back-to-back terms. The main contractor stays liable to the employer for its subcontractor (Article 832). A subcontractor cannot claim from the employer what the main contractor is owed unless the main contractor has referred it to the employer (Article 833).
  • Security. Performance, advance-payment and retention guarantees: amount, expiry, demand conditions.
  • Change and delay. Variation orders, notices, delay damages. A court may reduce agreed compensation that the debtor proves excessive (Article 340).
  • Termination. An employer may end the contract before completion but must pay expenses, work done and lost profit, which a court may reduce (Article 836).
  • Consultants. An engineer who only designs answers for design defects; one who supervises is jointly liable for execution defects (Article 822).
  • Around the project. Supply and plant-hire terms, joint-venture agreements, settlement agreements.

Checklist before you sign a legal retainer with a construction lawyer in the UAE

  • Check the provider's UAE licence. Onshore court representation is for lawyers on the Roll of Practising Lawyers (Article 6 of Federal Decree-Law No. (34) of 2022); legal consultants advise and draft.
  • Get the scope in writing: document types and volume.
  • Ask whether FIDIC-based contracts and Arabic-language documents are covered.
  • Read the exclusions: onshore litigation, tax, audits.
  • Confirm how arbitration support is priced.
  • Confirm the fee, VAT and what counts as extra work.
  • Ask for the response time, who reviews each document and how AI is used.
  • Ask about conflicts with your counterparties.
  • Check the term and how to cancel.

FAQ

What is construction law?

The rules on building contracts, licensing, site safety and disputes. Building construction law in the UAE draws on the Civil Transactions Law, emirate rules and the contract.

Is FIDIC law in the UAE?

No. FIDIC publishes standard forms of contract, so "FIDIC law" or "FIDIC construction law" is shorthand. FIDIC law and practice under UAE onshore law means applying those forms subject to mandatory rules such as Article 823 of the Civil Transactions Law, which voids clauses limiting the ten-year liability.

Does a retainer cover construction disputes?

Not the dispute itself. The construction legal services included are contract review, drafting, non-binding memos and research. Where construction law and arbitration meet, arbitration is scoped separately, like DIFC and ADGM court disputes. Onshore court claims, the work of construction litigation lawyers or construction dispute lawyers admitted to plead, are not included.
