Why healthcare companies in the UAE need ongoing legal support

UAE medical law is mostly federal, but Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Sharjah have their own health authorities, and Dubai and Abu Dhabi add their own laws (u.ae).

  • Licences attach to premises and to people. No one may establish, operate or manage a private health facility without a health authority licence, free zones included (Federal Law No. 4 of 2015, Articles 2–3). Physicians need a personal licence; practising without one carries fines from AED 100,000 to AED 2 million and, where the person is also unqualified, possible imprisonment (Federal Law No. 5 of 2019, Articles 4, 25 and 27).
  • Liability insurance is compulsory. Under the medical liability law, the profession may not be practised without insurance against civil liability for medical errors, and the facility owner pays the premiums (Federal Decree-Law No. 4 of 2016, Articles 25–26).
  • Health data stays in the country unless an exception applies. Data on health services provided in the UAE may be stored, processed or transferred abroad only in cases set by a health authority decision. Breaches carry a fine of AED 500,000–700,000; records are kept for at least 25 years (Federal Law No. 2 of 2019, Articles 13, 20 and 24).
  • Advertising needs a licence first. Health advertisements need a prior licence under Cabinet Resolution No. 7 of 2007, as amended (also in MOHAP's legislation list). MOHAP offers that licence as a service; Dubai fines advertising a facility's services without DHA approval (Executive Council Resolution No. 49 of 2024). Check the licensing body with your emirate's health authority. Advertising a medical product needs Emirates Drug Establishment approval (Federal Decree-Law No. 38 of 2024, Article 44).

The legal work that comes up every month

Much of it is contract work of the kind contract lawyers in Dubai handle for any business. In healthcare law, many clauses also have a rule behind them.

  • Employment contracts for licensed professionals: licence-dependent start dates, restrictive covenants, medical leave wording.
  • Provider agreements with insurers and third-party administrators: tariffs, claim rejections, audit clauses (the business side of what medical insurance lawyers do).
  • Equipment, supplier and distributor contracts.
  • Software, cloud and telehealth agreements with data-location and confidentiality clauses.
  • Marketing agency and influencer agreements that say who obtains advertising approval.
  • Leases, management agreements and visiting-doctor arrangements.

Checklist before you sign a legal retainer

  • The legal retainer agreement lists the included work, not only the fee.
  • Exclusions are written down: court litigation, tax, compliance audits.
  • You know the monthly volume the fee assumes.
  • The response time to a request is stated.
  • A lawyer reviews every document; use of AI tools is disclosed.
  • Confidentiality terms cover patient information.
  • The adviser has no conflict with your main insurer.
  • Cancellation terms are clear.

FAQ

What do medical lawyers do for a clinic or hospital?

For a business, a medical lawyer, hospital lawyer or medical legal advisor mostly handles contracts and regulatory questions. Defending malpractice claims in the onshore courts is outside the REGULEX subscription.

What does the medical liability law in the UAE require from a facility?

Federal Decree-Law No. 4 of 2016 Concerning Medical Liability, as amended, requires insurance against civil liability for medical errors, paid for by the facility owner (Articles 25–26). Medical Liability Committees decide whether a medical error occurred; compensation claims are not accepted before referral to them (Article 18).

Which UAE medical insurance law applies to employees?

In Dubai, Law No. 11 of 2013, the DHA insurance law, makes employers enrol employees and bear the cost, free zones included (Articles 4 and 10). The Abu Dhabi medical insurance law is Law No. 23 of 2005: employers cover employees and, within the limits of Article 5, their families. In the other five emirates, employers have had to buy a policy to issue or renew a private-sector employee's residency permit since 1 January 2025 (MOHRE).

Is there a universal health care law in the UAE?

The official sources cited here describe health insurance laws emirate by emirate; none is titled a universal health care law. On 19 May 2026 the state news agency WAM reported a presidential directive to adopt a national healthcare system for citizens, underpinned by a national health insurance scheme covering all emirates (WAM).

What is medical leave as per UAE law?

Under the federal private-sector labour law (DIFC and ADGM not covered), an employee who has passed probation may take up to 90 days of sick leave a year: 15 days on full pay, 30 on half pay, the rest unpaid. The employee informs the employer within three business days and submits a medical report (Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021, Article 31).
