Why media and entertainment companies in the UAE need ongoing legal support

A new regulator. Federal Decree-Law No. 11 of 2025 established the National Media Authority (NMA) from 1 January 2026. It replaced the UAE Media Council, the National Media Office and the Emirates News Agency (Article 13). Earlier resolutions stay in force until replaced (Article 16).

UAE media law reaches most content businesses. Federal Decree-Law No. 55 of 2023 Regulating Media lists as media activities broadcasting (including IPTV, OTT and video on demand), video games, films, newspapers and publications, book fairs, digital media and photography (Article 8). Licences and permits are issued under Article 5.

A free-zone licence does not remove federal content rules. Free-zone licensees must meet the media content standards, and activity outside the zone falls under the laws in force in the State (Article 6). The regulator may inspect media businesses in free zones (Article 22).

Content standards carry penalties. Article 17 lists the standards; the Executive Regulation adds more, including the age rating system (Article 28). Administrative fines run from AED 1,000 to AED 1,000,000, doubled on repetition up to AED 2,000,000, with closure of up to six months, renewable (Article 23).

Music licensing is changing. A Ministry of Economy and Tourism press release of 11 August 2026 announced a Collective Management in Music Guide under Ministerial Resolution No. 136 of 2026, with fees from the start of December 2026. The resolution's text was not on the Ministry's website on 18 November 2026.

The legal work that comes up every month

  • Content licensing and distribution. Territory, term, platforms, exclusivity, delivery and edits for local standards. Under the copyright law, a transfer or licence of economic rights must be in writing and state the right, purpose, duration and place; the author keeps every right not expressly assigned (Article 9).
  • Production and commissioning agreements. Unless agreed otherwise in writing, economic rights in work an employee creates for the employer's business belong to the employer, and copyright in work made for another person is attributed to that person (Article 28).
  • Talent and performer agreements. Performers hold their own economic rights (Article 17). A person who is photographed or recorded must authorise publication unless agreed otherwise, with exceptions such as public events (Article 45).
  • Music. Composer, synchronisation, recording and publishing deals. A company that would look for a music lawyer for each deal can route these contracts through the retainer.
  • Publishing. An author's disposal of all future work is void; an agreement may cover up to ten future works (Article 15; Article 18 of the Executive Regulations).
  • Locations and shoots. Private locations need the owner's no-objection certificate in Dubai and a location agreement in Abu Dhabi.
  • Channels and creators. Social media law issues: creator agreements, takedown requests, moderation.

FAQ

What is the UAE media law in force in 2026?

Federal Decree-Law No. 55 of 2023 Regulating Media, in force since 1 December 2023. Its Executive Regulation is Cabinet Resolution No. 68 of 2024. Since 1 January 2026 the federal regulator is the National Media Authority, under Federal Decree-Law No. 11 of 2025.

What does UAE law on social media mean for a media company's own channels?

The federal legislation platform lists no single statute called a "UAE social media law". Social media laws in the UAE sit mainly in the media law, the cybercrimes law and the child-safety rules. Whoever manages a site or account answers for publishing content that breaches media content standards (Article 19, cybercrimes law). As to UAE social media influencer law, individuals who advertise generally need the NMA Advertiser Permit.

Does a venue or broadcaster need a music licence?

According to the Ministry's press release, the Guide covers, among others, restaurants, cafés, malls, hotels, airlines, radio stations and television channels, with fees collected by the Emirates Music Rights Association and Music Nation. Whether a given business must pay depends on the resolution's text: check with a lawyer.

Can a retainer replace a media lawyer or social media lawyer?

It covers the contract, memo and research part of that work; litigation before the onshore courts is not included.

What is a retainer fee for a lawyer?

In this guide, a fixed periodic fee for an agreed scope of legal work. In media and entertainment law that scope is contracts, memos and research.
