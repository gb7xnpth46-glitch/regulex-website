Why AI and data companies in the UAE need ongoing legal support

Three regimes. The federal personal data protection law, Federal Decree by Law No. 45 of 2021 (the PDPL), has been in force since 2 January 2022. It excludes companies in free zones that have their own data protection legislation; DIFC entities follow the DIFC data protection law and ADGM entities follow the Data Protection Regulations 2021. These are the three general data protection laws UAE companies work under, and one group can fall under all three.

A federal law whose detail sits in regulations. The PDPL leaves breach-notification periods and transfer conditions to Executive Regulations, and penalties to a Cabinet decision (Articles 9, 23 and 26). On 14 November 2026 the official federal legislation platform listed neither for it. Article 29 allows six months from the issue of the Executive Regulations to regularise, a period the Cabinet may extend.

A regulator in transition. The PDPL names the UAE Data Office, created by Federal Decree by Law No. 44 of 2021. On 14 June 2026 the UAE Cabinet's website reported the approval of an Artificial Intelligence and Data Authority, reporting to the Cabinet, that is to bring together the functions of the Data Office, the AI Office and TDRA's digital government sector. The announcement does not mention the PDPL, and on 14 November 2026 the official legislation platform listed no law establishing the Authority. The sources opened for this guide do not say whether the Data Office's PDPL role has passed to the Authority.

AI rules and policies sit in several instruments. Artificial intelligence and law meet in Article 18 of the PDPL (objection to automated decisions, with exceptions; human review on request), in DIFC Regulation 10, in the national AI Charter and in Cyber Security Council policy.

Sector and criminal rules. Health and banking data have their own laws. A breach of privacy law can also be a crime: the cybercrimes law punishes processing personal data in violation of legislation (Article 13).

The legal work that comes up every month

  • Customer contracts: SaaS and API terms, data processing agreements, service levels and liability caps. Enterprise buyers ask how the terms line up with international data protection laws.
  • Vendor and model contracts: cloud hosting, model licences, data licences, rights in training data and outputs.
  • Transfers abroad: PDPL Articles 22 and 23 provide for transfers to countries with data protection legislation, or under a contract, explicit consent or other listed grounds. The DIFC and ADGM publish adequacy lists and standard clauses.
  • Product questions: a memo on whether a scoring feature triggers Article 18 or DIFC Regulation 10, or whether health data may be hosted abroad.
  • Policies and notices: privacy notices, website terms, internal AI-use rules, NDAs.

FAQ

What is privacy law?

Privacy law, or information privacy law, is the set of rules on how organisations collect, use, share and secure information about identifiable people. Privacy laws in the UAE include the federal PDPL, the DIFC and ADGM regimes, sector laws and criminal provisions such as Article 44 of the cybercrimes law on invasion of privacy.

Data protection law Dubai: which regime applies?

A Dubai mainland company generally falls under the federal PDPL. A DIFC entity falls under DIFC Law No. 5 of 2020. On the wording of Article 2(2)(g), companies in other free zones fall under the PDPL unless the zone has its own data legislation. Which regime applies to a particular company depends on its facts and is a point to check with a lawyer.

Personal data protection law UAE: have the Executive Regulations been issued?

On 14 November 2026 the official platform showed the law as last updated on 20 September 2021, and its related legislation list contained no Executive Regulations.

Is there an artificial intelligence law in the UAE?

A search of the official legislation platform for "artificial intelligence" on 14 November 2026 returned 11 instruments that mention AI and none dedicated to it. On 18 May 2026 the Cabinet approved the drafting of a federal law on smart health applications and AI in the health sector, as the Government of Dubai Media Office reported. Binding rules that touch AI include Article 18 of the PDPL and DIFC Regulation 10. The Charter is published as a public policy.
