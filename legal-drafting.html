Overview

Essential documents for your daily business operations

REGULEX drafts and reviews the full range of legal documents your business needs — from standard NDAs to complex multi-party agreements. Every document is tailored to your specific commercial context and regulatory environment.

When to involve us

When you need a document drafted

  • You are starting talks with a partner and need an NDA or a memorandum of understanding.
  • You need a contract with a supplier, a distributor or a service provider.
  • An existing contract has to be changed by an amendment, an appendix or a side letter.
  • The board or the shareholders have to record a decision in resolutions or minutes.
  • You are engaging an adviser, or taking on a client, and need terms of engagement.

FAQ

Questions about legal drafting services

Which documents does REGULEX draft?

Memorandums of understanding, non-disclosure agreements, commercial contracts, supplementary agreements, corporate resolutions and terms of engagement.

Does REGULEX review documents prepared by the other side?

Yes. REGULEX drafts and reviews legal documents; a review comes back as a redline with comments.

Are legal drafting services covered by the subscription?

Yes. Contract review, redlines with comments and drafting of new agreements are the core of the legal services subscription, from USD 990 per month + 5% VAT. Notarisation and apostille are not included.

How do we start?

Send a short description of the matter to info@regulex-legal.com. REGULEX responds to each request within 24 hours. Scope and fees are agreed before work starts.
