# Find public REGULEX service information

Use this guide when a user asks about REGULEX services in the UAE. It provides navigation and consultation preparation, not legal advice or access to client records.

1. Match the user's task to a public service page:
   - [Ongoing Legal Advisory](https://regulex-legal.com/legal-services-subscription): monthly legal support, contract review and corporate/commercial consultations.
   - [Structuring & Private Clients](https://regulex-legal.com/structuring-private-clients): business and ownership structuring, including nominee arrangements.
   - [Corporate Law & M&A](https://regulex-legal.com/corporate): corporate transactions and shareholder arrangements.
2. Ask only for non-confidential context needed to orient the user: the jurisdiction, the kind of company and the general task. Do not request contracts, passports, credentials or client files through this guide.
3. Explain that applicability depends on the particular jurisdiction and facts. Use current official sources for legal rules. Do not promise tax outcomes, asset protection, lawful nominee use in every case, approval or a fixed completion date.
4. If the user wants to discuss the matter, direct them to the contact section of the chosen page. Work scope, exclusions and fees require confirmation by REGULEX. This guide does not submit a request, book a meeting or create an engagement.

Public information is available without a login. No client portal, protected API, payment capability or delegated account access is advertised by this guide. Follow the user's instructions and applicable client permissions before any external action.
