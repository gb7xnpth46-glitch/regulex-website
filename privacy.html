Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Information We Collect

When you contact REGULEX through our website or direct communication channels, we collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us. This includes:

  • Your full name and professional title
  • Email address and phone number
  • Company name and industry sector
  • Details about your legal inquiry or service requirements
  • Any other information you choose to share with us

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely for legitimate business purposes:

  • To respond to your legal inquiries and provide consultations
  • To deliver our legal services and maintain attorney-client relationships
  • To communicate with you about engagements, invoices, and legal matters
  • To improve our services based on your feedback
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

We do not use your information for marketing purposes without your explicit consent, nor do we sell or share your data with third parties for their commercial benefit.

Data Protection and Security

REGULEX implements comprehensive security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our security protocols include:

  • Encrypted communication channels for sensitive information
  • Secure file storage and access controls
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Confidentiality agreements with all staff and contractors

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses minimal cookies, primarily for analytics purposes to understand how visitors interact with our site. We use these insights to improve user experience and website functionality. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Our use of cookies does not involve personal identification.

Third-Party Services

REGULEX uses carefully selected third-party services to enhance our website and operations:

  • Website hosting and contact form (Netlify): Our website is hosted on Netlify. When you submit the contact form, the details you enter (name, email, phone, the service you are interested in and your message) are processed and stored by Netlify Forms so that we can respond to your request. Our fonts are served from our own website, not from a third-party font service.
  • Analytics Services: We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to measure website traffic and user behavior, which helps us optimize our online presence. These tools load only after you interact with the page.

We ensure all third-party service providers maintain appropriate data protection standards. We do not share your personal information with these services unless necessary for their function, and we have agreements in place requiring them to protect your data in accordance with this policy.

Your Rights

As an individual whose data we process, you have the following rights in accordance with UAE data protection laws and international standards:

  • Right of Access: You can request access to all personal information we hold about you
  • Right of Correction: You can request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right of Deletion: You can request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request your information in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below. We will respond to all requests within 30 days in compliance with applicable data protection regulations.
